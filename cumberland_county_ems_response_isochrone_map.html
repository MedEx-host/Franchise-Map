Presentation Defense Points

1. Strategic Highway Proximity Stationing at 207 Airport Rd provides immediate ramp access to I-95 (< 2 minutes), eliminating residential choke points when mobilizing toward North or South county sectors.
2. Level III Trauma Coverage Cape Fear Valley Medical Center (the region's primary trauma & acute destination) is within the ~8-minute turnout envelope along Owen Dr, minimizing turn-around and bed turnaround delays.
3. Non-Adverse Interlocking Proves compliance with Board Standard §(d)(2): The service footprint smoothly meshes with existing Cumberland County EMS zones without cannabilizing response units in western/northern suburban grids.
